Questions people ask before booking

Can't see yours? Call 020 3621 8527, Monday to Saturday, 8AM to 7PM.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

It depends on the size of the kitchen and the scope of work, so we don't quote a standard figure. Before work starts you receive a work programme plan, so you know the timeline from day one. Stone worktops are templated once the cabinets are installed, then cut off-site and fitted, which adds a short wait. Your estimator will talk you through timings at the free site visit.

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in London?

Every kitchen is different, so we don't publish set prices. The cost depends on the size of the room, any layout changes, the units, worktops and appliances you choose, and the electrical and plumbing work involved. After one of our estimators has visited your home, you receive a clear, itemised proposal, so you know exactly what you are paying for before committing to anything.

Do you design the kitchen?

Your kitchen is designed by a qualified kitchen designer. If you already have one, we work from their design. If not, we can arrange a free design appointment with one of our kitchen suppliers, Howdens, Magnet or Kutchenhaus, whose designers create a full 3D design of your kitchen before anything is ordered. We then take care of supply and installation end to end.

Do I need to supply my own units, worktops and appliances?

No. If you'd like, we handle it all: we order everything on your behalf, check deliveries, store materials securely until your start date, and deal with any damaged items, exchanges and returns. You choose what you want and we take care of the rest.

Do you offer fit-only kitchen installation?

No. We only take on complete kitchen renovations: strip-out, electrics, plumbing, cabinets, worktops, splashback, tiling, flooring and appliances, managed as one project with one point of contact. That is how we can stand behind the finished kitchen as a whole, not just one part of it.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. All workmanship is covered by a 12-month guarantee against faulty labour as standard on every project. If anything falls short of your expectations within the first year, we will return and put it right at no additional cost to you. Materials are covered by their manufacturers' warranties, and the work is backed by our £5M Public Liability Insurance.

Do you use subcontractors or your own team?

Both. Our kitchen fitters are employed by A&G and work on our projects only, so the same team is in your home from strip-out to handover. Specialist trades are different: electrical work is carried out by NICEIC certified electricians, gas work by Gas Safe registered engineers and stone worktops by specialist stonemasons, all partners we work with regularly. We coordinate them, so you deal with one point of contact throughout.

Do you charge a fee for quoting, or use a matching service?

No. The site visit and the written proposal are free, and there is no obligation to proceed. If you contact us directly there is also no agency or matching-platform commission in your quote. Those platforms are free for you to use because they charge the builder a percentage of every job won, and that cost has to be recovered somewhere. Our direct quotes are typically 10–15% lower than the same project priced through a matching platform such as The Page or Beams, because the commission is not there to pass on.

What areas of London do you cover?

We cover Greater London inside the M25. If you are unsure whether we reach you, call 020 3621 8527 or fill in the enquiry form and we will confirm.

A clear, itemised proposal is then prepared and sent to you for review, with no obligation to proceed.
